MAPLE RIDGE OUTFITTERS - RETURN AND EXCHANGE POLICY
Effective: January 1, 2025. Owner: Customer Experience Team.

1. STANDARD RETURN WINDOW
Customers may return unused merchandise within 45 days of purchase with a receipt or order number. Items must be in original condition with tags attached. Returns are accepted at any Maple Ridge store or by mail for online orders.

2. LOYALTY MEMBER EXTENSION
Gold tier loyalty members receive an extended return window of 60 days. Silver and Bronze members follow the standard 45-day window.

3. CATEGORY EXCEPTIONS
Electronics (including GPS watches, power banks, headlamps and satellite messengers) must be returned within 30 days and must include all accessories and original packaging.
Tents that have been set up outdoors may be returned only if they are free of damage; a 15% restocking fee applies to opened tents returned after 14 days.
Final sale and clearance items marked "FINAL" are not eligible for return or exchange.

4. REFUNDS
Refunds are issued to the original method of payment. Card refunds are processed within 5 to 7 business days after the return is received. Purchases made with gift cards are refunded to a new gift card. Original shipping fees are non-refundable unless the item was defective or shipped in error.

5. EXCHANGES
Size and colour exchanges are free of charge within the return window. If the replacement item is out of stock, the customer may choose a refund or a store credit valid for 12 months.

6. DEFECTIVE PRODUCTS AND WARRANTY
Products with manufacturing defects are covered by a one-year Maple Ridge warranty from the date of purchase. Footwear defects reported within 90 days are replaced without inspection.

7. RETURNS WITHOUT RECEIPT
Returns without a receipt or order number are limited to store credit at the item's lowest selling price in the previous 60 days, with a maximum of three receipt-less returns per customer per year.
