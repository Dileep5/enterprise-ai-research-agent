# Maple Ridge Outfitters - Store Operations Handbook (2025 edition)

## Opening hours
Stores are open Monday to Saturday from 10:00 to 20:00 and Sunday from 11:00 to 18:00. Holiday hours are published by the regional manager at least two weeks in advance.

## Inventory management
Each store performs a weekly cycle count every Monday before opening, covering at least 20% of active SKUs so that every SKU is counted at least once every five weeks.

A product must be reordered when its quantity on hand falls below its reorder point. Store managers submit replenishment requests through the central replenishment system by 14:00 on Tuesday and Thursday; requests received after the cutoff are processed in the next cycle.

Stock-outs of top-20 SKUs must be escalated to the regional inventory planner within 24 hours. Inventory variance above 3% of counted value for a category triggers a full recount of that category within 48 hours.

## Safety stock
Safety stock for seasonal categories (Tents, Sleeping Bags and Winter footwear) is raised by 25% between April and August for camping gear and between October and January for winter products.

## Cash handling and loss prevention
Registers are reconciled at every shift change. Cash variances above $20 must be reported to the store manager the same day. High-value electronics are stored in locked display cases.

## Customer service standards
Every customer should be greeted within 30 seconds of entering the store. Online order pickups must be ready within 2 hours of the order confirmation during opening hours.

## Incident reporting
Workplace safety incidents must be logged in the incident system within 12 hours and reviewed by the health and safety committee monthly.
